Setup

Anything OpenAI-compatible. Your key stays in this browser's storage on this device and is sent only to the endpoint below — no server of ours is in the path. A shared phone is the wrong place to keep a key.

Where your audio goes

Three different paths, and they do not have the same answer. This is stated rather than smoothed over, because "it's private" would be a claim I cannot check for all three.

Speaking (Clementine's voice) — stays on your phone. Your device's own installed voices are used, and only those. If there is no on-device voice, she stays silent rather than fall back to a network one.

The talk button — depends on your browser, and I cannot verify it. It uses the browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome that sends audio to Google. In Safari it is Apple's recogniser, and whether it runs on-device or on Apple's servers is not something this page can determine. Treat it as leaving the device unless you know otherwise.

The keyboard's microphone key — the private option. Tap Type, then the 🎤 on the iOS keyboard. That is the system dictation, on-device for supported languages, and the audio never reaches this page at all. Slower to use; the one whose answer is clean.

Your words to the model always leave. Whatever is transcribed is sent to the endpoint you configured. That part is the same either way.
